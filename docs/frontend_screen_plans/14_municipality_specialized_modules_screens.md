# Screen Plan 14: 10 Municipality-Specific Specialized Screens

> **Backend Phase:** Phase 11 (Municipality-Specific Modules)  
> **Target Audience:** Specialized Municipal Officers (Sanitary Inspectors, Health Officers, Town Planning Engineers, Fleet Managers)

---

## 1. Solid Waste Management Screen (`/municipality/solid-waste`)

### Purpose
Tracks municipal garbage bin fill levels, door-to-door waste collection routes, and sanitation worker logs.
- **Key Features:** GIS Map of public dumpsters with fill-level pins (Green/Yellow/Red), Daily route completion progress bar, Door-to-door collection vehicle log, Sanitary worker attendance counter.

---

## 2. Road & Pothole Monitoring Screen (`/municipality/potholes`)

### Purpose
Monitors road condition ratings, pothole complaints, repair work orders, and road resurfacing projects.
- **Key Features:** Interactive GIS Road Pothole Map, Severity Level badges (Minor, Severe, Critical), Work Order linking for road patching, Side-by-side Before/After repair photo comparison.

---

## 3. Drainage & Flood Control Screen (`/municipality/drainage`)

### Purpose
Manages storm drain desilting schedules, flood vulnerability hotspots, and monsoon emergency preparedness.
- **Key Features:** Storm drain desilting progress tracker, Low-lying flood hazard zone map, Monsoon emergency pump station readiness log, Canal cleaning inspection record.

---

## 4. Parks & Open Spaces Screen (`/municipality/parks`)

### Purpose
Oversees municipal parks, playgrounds, public gardens, equipment maintenance, and entry records.
- **Key Features:** Park asset inventory (play equipment, benches, fountain pumps), Mowing & watering schedule tracker, Equipment safety inspection log, Public entry footfall logger.

---

## 5. Public Health & Sanitation Screen (`/municipality/health`)

### Purpose
Tracks vector control fogging drives, sanitation campaigns, and food business hygiene inspections.
- **Key Features:** Vector control / mosquito fogging schedule map, Sanitation campaign log, Commercial food establishment hygiene score audit, Public health outbreak alert logger.

---

## 6. Building Permit & Plan Approval Screen (`/municipality/building-permits`)

### Purpose
Processes citizen building plan applications, blueprint reviews, NOC clearances, and site inspection schedules.
- **Key Features:** Blueprint PDF viewer, Multi-department NOC approval checklist (Fire, Traffic, EB), Site visit inspection scheduler, Structural safety sign-off.

---

## 7. Birth & Death Registration Screen (`/municipality/vital-events`)

### Purpose
Manages vital statistics registration, hospital feeds, certificate generation, and QR code verification.
- **Key Features:** Searchable birth/death record registry, Hospital reporting electronic feed, Certificate generation with digital seal, QR code instant verification printer.

---

## 8. Vehicle Fleet & Fuel Tracking Screen (`/municipality/fleet`)

### Purpose
Real-time tracking of municipal vehicles (garbage trucks, water tankers, tractors), fuel consumption, and maintenance schedules.
- **Key Features:** Live GPS vehicle location tracking map, Fuel log entry form (Liters, Odometer reading, Total Cost), Driver assignment history, Vehicle service due reminder cards.

---

## 9. Encroachment Removal Screen (`/municipality/encroachment`)

### Purpose
Logs unauthorized public land encroachments, survey reports, eviction notices, and removal drives.
- **Key Features:** Encroachment GIS map, Survey report uploader, Eviction notice generator (`Form I & II`), Police coordination status tracker, Demolition drive log.

---

## 10. Cemetery & Crematorium Management Screen (`/municipality/cemeteries`)

### Purpose
Manages municipal cemetery/crematorium bookings, fuel/wood inventory, and cremation certificates.
- **Key Features:** Burial slot & crematorium booking calendar, Fuel/wood stock inventory gauge, Maintenance log, Cremation certificate issuing module.
