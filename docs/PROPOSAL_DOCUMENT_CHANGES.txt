Module 1: Real-Time Conversational AI Voice Agent & Dual IVR Helpline

• 1.1 Conversational AI Voice Agent (Primary Helpline — 04440115043): Dedicated municipal helpline powered by Exotel and conversational AI models. Features natural, human-like two-way spoken dialogue in colloquial Tamil, Tanglish, and English without rigid menu trees.

Module 2: QR-Enabled Asset Management & Instant Citizen Reporting

• 2.1 Digital Asset Registry: Unique digital identity for poles, pipelines, valves, borewells, OHTs, GLRs, sumps, smart bins, roads, drains, parks, and municipal vehicles.
• 2.2 Weatherproof QR Tagging: Durable physical stickers linked directly to asset ID, GIS coordinates, inspection logs, and maintenance histories.
• 2.3 No-App Scan-to-Report: Instant mobile browser scan pre-loaded with asset metadata, fault selection, and photo upload without app installation.
• 2.4 Anonymous Tracking: Secure tracking URL and lifecycle statuses: Registered → Assigned → In Progress → Work Completed → Verified → Closed.
