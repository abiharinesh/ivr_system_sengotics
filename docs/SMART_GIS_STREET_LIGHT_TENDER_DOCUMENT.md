### 5.1 Project-Specific Service Level Agreements (SLAs)
1. **99%+ Street Lighting Network Uptime**:
   - The contractor must guarantee that at least **99.0%** of all street lights in Mettupalayam Municipality remain fully operational every night.
   - Uptime is calculated monthly as:
     $$\text{Uptime \%} = \left(1 - \frac{\text{Cumulative Dark Spot Outage Pole Hours}}{\text{Total Network Poles} \times 12 \text{ Operating Hours} \times \text{Days in Month}}\right) \times 100$$
   - A drop below 99.0% will attract a penalty of 0.5% of the monthly O&M bill for every 0.5% decrement, up to a maximum deduction of 10%.
2. **Defect Rectification Timelines**:
   - **Minor Faults (Fused LED, Driver Replacement, Loose Wire)**: Must be physically rectified within **12 hours** of complaint registration.
   - **Major Faults (Feeder Line Break, Underground Cable Fault, Phase Imbalance)**: Must be rectified within **24 hours**.

### 5.2 Mandatory $\le$50m Geo-Fencing Enforcement & Anti-Fraud Audit
1. **Anti-Fraud Geo-Lock**: The Field Electrician Mobile Application must enforce a hard hardware GPS lock. The electrician can only mark a ticket 'Resolved' if their live device GNSS coordinates are verified to be within **$\le$ 50 meters** of the surveyed pole's registered WGS-84 coordinates.
2. **Live EXIF Timestamped Photography**: The app must require a live photo capture of the illuminated pole with embedded tamper-proof GPS and UTC timestamp EXIF data. Gallery uploads and GPS spoofing are strictly prohibited.
3. **Audit Penalty**: Any ticket resolved outside the 50m perimeter or with duplicate/spoofed imagery will be automatically voided, re-assigned to the field, and penalized at **₹500 per fraudulent occurrence** deducted from the contractor's monthly payment.

### 5.3 24/7 Dual-Helpline AI Voice Telephony SLAs
1. **Service Availability**: The primary Conversational AI Agent (`044-40115043`) and DTMF IVR (`044-40115434`) must maintain **99.5% telecommunication availability**.
2. **Concurrent Channels**: The telephony infrastructure must support at least **30 concurrent calls** without busy signals.
3. **Dialect Accuracy**: The Tamil Speech-to-Text (ASR) engine must achieve $\ge 90\%$ recognition accuracy across Kongu, Chennai, Madurai, and Delta colloquial spoken dialects.
4. **Outage Penalty**: Telephony downtime exceeding 30 minutes in a single day will be penalized at ₹1,000 per hour of unannounced downtime.

### 5.4 Annual QR Sticker Renewal & Physical Audit
1. **Material Durability**: QR stickers must endure continuous exposure to rain, high humidity, and direct UV sunlight.
2. **Annual Renewal Cycle**: As part of the 3-Year comprehensive O&M, the contractor shall conduct an annual inspection of 100% of the poles and replace damaged, faded, vandalized, or missing QR tags free of extra cost to the Municipality.

### 5.5 Payment Milestones & O&M Billing Breakdown
1. **Milestone 1 (40% of Capex)**: Successful completion of 100% door-to-door GNSS sub-meter survey and database ingestion of all 12,500 poles.
2. **Milestone 2 (30% of Capex)**: Physical supply, fabrication, and affixing of 80×80mm weatherproof QR tags on all surveyed poles with inspection sign-off.
3. **Milestone 3 (30% of Capex)**: Full deployment and commissioning of 24/7 Dual-Helpline AI Voice Telephony, Flutter Mobile/Web Suite, and completion of 30 days of seamless pilot testing.
4. **O&M Payments (Quarterly)**: Operation & Maintenance charges shall be paid in **12 equal quarterly installments** over the 36-month period, subject to SLA compliance certificates and uptime calculations signed by the Municipal Electrical Engineer.
