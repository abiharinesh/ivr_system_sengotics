Section I: Notice Inviting Tender (NIT)

1.1 Invitation: The Commissioner, Mettupalayam Municipality (hereinafter termed the 'Procuring Entity'), on behalf of the Governor of Tamil Nadu, invites eligible and competent technology providers and system integrators to submit bids through the online e-procurement portal https://tntenders.gov.in for the Turnkey Implementation, Deployment, and 3-Year Comprehensive Operation & Maintenance of Smart GIS Street Light & Electric Pole Infrastructure Management System across all 33 municipal wards.

1.2 The Project: Addresses critical municipal bottlenecks including unreported dark spots, digital exclusion of senior and rural citizens, and fraudulent complaint closures. It delivers end-to-end door-to-door sub-meter GNSS pole survey, 80×80mm weatherproof retroreflective QR plates, 24/7 dual-helpline AI telephony (conversational Tamil ASR + DTMF IVR), multi-platform Flutter applications (Admin command center, field electrician mobile with mandatory ≤50m geo-fencing lock, and citizen QR PWA/app), with strict 99%+ street light uptime enforcement.

Section II & III: Instructions to Bidders & AITB

2.1 Submission System: Bids must be submitted online in two covers on https://tntenders.gov.in. Cover 1 consists of the Techno-Commercial Bid including Bid Securing Declaration (Form 7), Eligibility Declarations (Form 1.2), Schedule of Requirements Compliance (Form 2), Technical Specifications Compliance (Form 3), Qualification Documents (Form 4 & 4.1), and Integrity Pact (Form 8). Cover 2 comprises the Price Schedule (BOQ Excel sheet).

2.2 Price Basis: The bid price shall be quoted entirely in Indian Rupees (INR) and shall remain firm and fixed for the 90-day implementation period and the 36-month O&M cycle. No price variation or exchange rate escalation shall be admitted.

2.3 Composite Evaluation: Financial evaluation shall be conducted on the basis of the combined turnkey landed cost of all five schedules (Survey, QR Tags, Telephony, Software Suite, and 3-Year O&M) to establish the lowest evaluated responsive bidder (L-1).

7.2 24/7 Dual-Helpline AI Voice Telephony & Regional Tamil Dialect Processing

The system shall provide zero-barrier citizen redressal via dual telephony channels:

  • Primary Conversational AI Agent (044-40115043): Equipped with deep neural Tamil Speech Recognition (ASR) tuned to colloquial regional Tamil Nadu dialects:
    • Kongu Tamil (Coimbatore, Mettupalayam, Tirupur, Erode colloquialisms)
    • Chennai Tamil (Madras colloquial speech)
    • Madurai & Delta Tamil expressions
    Citizens speak freely without navigating numeric keypad menus. The voice agent recognizes intents, extracts pole and landmark tokens, queries the database, and responds naturally in Tamil.
  • Secondary DTMF Keypad IVR (044-40115434): Built for basic 2G feature phones and noisy rural conditions. The citizen dials the number and keys in the 3 or 4-digit pole number followed by hash (e.g. 101#) to log the ticket.
Sample Live Tamil AI Voice Redressal Dialogue:
குடிமகன்: "வணக்கம், காந்தி நகர் 3வது தெருவுல லைட் எரியல."
AI குரல் முகவர்: "உங்கள் புகார் பதிவு செய்யப்பட்டது. மின்கம்ப எண் 101-க்கு கள மின்சார பணியாளர் நியமிக்கப்பட்டுள்ளார்; இன்று மாலைக்குள் சரிசெய்யப்படும்."
