## 1. Executive Summary & Technology Advantage

Traditional methods of attaching metal plates with stainless steel strapping are labor-intensive and costly. Sengotics has engineered an optimized **Industrial Hybrid Chemical Bonding Technique** utilizing **1-Component Moisture-Curing Polyurethane (PU) Elastomeric Adhesive** combined with an **instant-tack structural acrylic core**:

1. **Zero Mechanical Damage to Utility Poles:** 100% compliant with electricity board (TANGEDCO) regulations prohibiting drilling into Pre-Stressed Concrete (PSC/RCC) utility poles.
2. **Permanent Structural Bond:** The industrial Polyurethane paste achieves a shear strength $> 3.4\text{ N/mm}^2$ ($> 34\text{ kg/cm}^2$), curing into an indestructible, waterproof rubber weld capable of withstanding heavy monsoon rains, tropical summer heat ($up to +80^\circ\text{C}$), and mechanical vibration.
3. **Instant Initial Grab:** A high-tack center core prevents the plate from slipping or sliding during initial application, allowing immediate mobile app verification and rapid field completion.
4. **Substantial Public Cost Savings:** Reduces the per-pole installation expenditure by **over 50%**, saving the civic body over **₹3,00,000** compared to traditional metal banding methods.

## 8. Technical Specifications of Materials

### 8.1 Heavy-Duty 1-Component Polyurethane (PU) Adhesive
- **Chemical Base:** 1-Component moisture-curing polyurethane prepolymer.
- **Consistency:** Non-sag, thixotropic heavy paste (does not drip or slump on vertical surfaces).
- **Skin Formation Time:** 35 – 50 minutes at $25^\circ\text{C}$ / 50% RH.
- **Curing Rate:** $\approx 3.5\text{ mm} / 24\text{ hours}$.
- **Shear Strength (ASTM D1002):** $> 3.4\text{ N/mm}^2$ ($> 34\text{ kg/cm}^2$).
- **Tensile Strength (ASTM D412):** $> 3.0\text{ N/mm}^2$.
- **Elongation at Break:** $> 500\%$ (provides elastic cushioning against traffic vibrations).
- **Service Temperature Range:** $-30^\circ\text{C}\text{ to }+80^\circ\text{C}$ (continuous outdoor thermal stability).
- **Chemical & Weather Resistance:** Completely impervious to fresh water, salt water, dilute acids, and UV sunlight radiation.

### 8.2 Rigid Waterproof Molded QR Asset Plates
- **Substrate Material:** High-Impact Polycarbonate (PC) / Outdoor Grade Acrylonitrile Butadiene Styrene (ABS).
- **Thickness & Dimensions:** $120\text{ mm} \times 80\text{ mm} \times 2.0\text{ mm} \pm 5\%$.
- **Printing Technology:** 1440 DPI Direct UV LED cured printing with protective lacquer overcoat.
- **Durability Rating:** Minimum 5 to 7 years outdoor legibility under direct sunlight.
