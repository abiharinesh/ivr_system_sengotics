1. Project Background & Executive Summary

Urban and Rural Local Bodies in Tamil Nadu manage extensive street lighting networks. However, civic administrations face critical operational bottlenecks: unreported dark spots, digital exclusion of elderly or rural citizens who cannot use text-based mobile apps, and fraudulent complaint closures where contractors mark repairs completed without physical field presence.

This project introduces a turnkey e-governance framework integrating Conversational AI Voice Redressal in Tamil and English, Weatherproof Retroreflective QR Asset Tags, Citizen Instant Scan Portals, Field Electrician Geo-Fencing (≤ 50m), and Executive I3C GIS Command Dashboards.

Core Objective for Tender Publication:

To procure a turnkey technology partner who will survey 100% of the municipal electric poles, install tamper-proof QR plates, operate 24/7 dual-helpline AI voice infrastructure, and provide geo-verified field enforcement to maintain 99%+ street light uptime.

2.1 Door-to-Door GIS Asset Survey & Retroreflective QR Tagging

  • Sub-Meter GNSS Survey: Field mapping of every street light pole capturing Latitude, Longitude, Pole Material (Tubular, PSC Concrete, Octagonal), Lamp Type (LED, SV), Wattage (20W, 40W, 70W, 120W), Feeder Phase, and bilingual Tamil/English landmark.
  • Weatherproof Anodized Aluminum Plates: Fabrication and physical installation of 150mm × 100mm retroreflective QR plates attached with Grade 304 Stainless Steel banding at a height of 2.5 meters.

2.2 24/7 Dual-Helpline AI Voice Telephony

  • Primary Conversational AI Agent (04440115043): Realistic natural Tamil speech understanding. Citizens simply speak (e.g. "பஸ் ஸ்டாண்ட் பக்கத்துல ஸ்ட்ரீட் லைட் எரியல"). The AI dynamically queries existing complaint statuses from PostgreSQL and speaks back live updates.
  • Secondary DTMF Keypad IVR (04440115434): Fast dialpad entry for basic feature phones. Citizen enters 3-digit pole ID (e.g., 101#) to instantly register complaints.
