Tender Concept & Scope Document (For Bidding Vendors)

Tender for Smart Street Lighting Infrastructure & Grievance Management System

Turnkey Implementation of Weatherproof QR Asset Tagging, 24/7 Conversational AI Voice Calling, Citizen Apps, and Geo-Fenced Workforce Verification

1. The Problem Statement (What Needs to Be Solved)

Municipalities and Town Panchayats manage thousands of street light poles across expanding wards, facing critical operational challenges:

1. Unreported Dark Spots & Delayed Redressal

Street lights often stay dark for days because civic bodies have no automated mechanism to detect failures, depending solely on slow manual reports.

2. Digital Exclusion of Citizens

Existing mobile grievance apps require smartphone typing, English literacy, and complex menus, cutting off elderly and rural citizens from reporting faults.

3. Fake Closures by Contractors

Contractors or electricians often mark tickets "Resolved" without physically visiting the pole, causing recurring complaints and wasted municipal funds.

4. Lack of Digitized Asset Registry

Municipalities lack accurate GIS coordinates, pole IDs, wattage, and feeder lines, making preventative maintenance and auditing impossible.

2. The Concept (How the Solution Works)

The proposed system integrates on-ground asset tagging, intelligent voice AI, and geo-verified field tracking into one seamless loop:

End-to-End Operational Workflow:
1. Citizen Scans QR / Calls AIInstant ticket logged in 30s
2. PostGIS Spatial MatchAuto-assigned to ward electrician
3. Field App ≤50m LockWorker must reach pole
4. Photo & OCR ClosureBefore/after proof + SMS to citizen

3. Core Solution Pillars

A. Weatherproof QR Code Asset Plates (Physical Tagging)

• Every pole is affixed with a durable, UV-resistant, waterproof QR plate at 2.2m height with a unique Pole Keypad ID and crypto QR code.
• How it works: Any citizen pointing their phone camera at the QR code opens an instant reporting page in their mobile browser—zero app download required. The pole number, ward, and location are automatically pre-filled.

B. 24/7 Dual-Helpline Voice System (Conversational AI + Keypad IVR)

• Primary Helpline (Conversational AI Agent): Citizens dial the helpline and speak naturally in colloquial Tamil, Tanglish, or English (e.g., "பேருந்து நிறுத்தம் பக்கத்துல விளக்கு எரியல"). The AI extracts the problem, logs the ticket, and dynamically speaks back live status updates on follow-up calls.
• Secondary Helpline (Keypad DTMF IVR): For basic 2G feature phones; caller dials the helpline and enters the 3-digit Pole ID on their keypad (e.g., 101#) to instantly log the outage.

C. Citizen Engagement Suite (Mobile App & Instant PWA)

• Instant Scan Web PWA: Lightweight camera scan portal for one-tap complaint submission without installing an app.
• Citizen Mobile App: Interactive street light map, personal complaint history, automated SMS/WhatsApp alerts with live tracking links upon repair.

D. Field Electrician App with Mandatory Geo-Fencing

• Mandatory ≤ 50-Meter Radius Lock: Ticket closure is strictly blocked unless the electrician’s phone GPS is within 50 meters of the physical pole coordinates.
• Live Camera Before/After Photo & OCR Proof: Electrician must capture a live photo of the repaired light; built-in OCR scans the pole number tag to ensure zero fake closures.
