1. Project Background & Executive Summary

Supply of safe, reliable, and equitable drinking water is the vital statutory responsibility of Local Bodies. However, urban and rural administrations face recurring operational challenges: Non-Revenue Water (NRW) losses from undetected underground pipe bursts, inequitable distribution caused by manual and unmonitored sluice valve operations, dry-running of borewell pumps, and slow response times to citizen water contamination or scarcity complaints.

This project establishes an integrated, smart water distribution governance ecosystem combining GIS Underground Pipeline Vector Mapping (GeoJSON LineStrings), Overhead Tank (OHT) & Sump Ultrasonic Level Telemetry, Sluice Valve State Management, LPS & Pressure Bar Telemetry, Conversational AI Voice Grievance Helpline in Tamil, and Geo-Fenced Plumber Mobile Verification (≤ 50m).

Core Objective for Tender Publication:

To appoint a turnkey technology and implementation partner who will map the entire underground drinking water network, install IoT reservoir level transmitters, operate 24/7 bilingual AI water grievance helpline, and provide geo-verified mobile tools for field plumbers and valve operators to ensure equitable, zero-waste water distribution.

2. Detailed Scope of Work (SOW)

1. GIS Pipeline Vector Mapping

Precision GNSS survey of drinking water mains and feeder pipelines. Vectorized as GeoJSON LineStrings with pipe diameter (50mm–600mm), material (HDPE, PVC, DI, CI), depth, and operating pressure.

2. Reservoir & Borewell Telemetry

Continuous monitoring of Overhead Tanks (OHT) and sumps via IP68 ultrasonic level sensors (0–100% capacity). Borewell motor operational status tracking (RUN / OFF / TRIP).

3. Sluice Valve & Flow Logging

Digital tracking of sluice valves (Open / Closed / Maintenance). Time-series flow rate logging (Liters/Sec) and pressure (Bars) with automated burst alerts upon sudden pressure drops.

4. 24/7 Conversational AI Voice Line

Dedicated helpline (04440115043) understanding colloquial Tamil for water leaks, contamination, low pressure, and tanker booking, with instant live status lookups.

2.1 Field Asset Capture & Mobile Plumber Workflow

  • Field Asset Capture Tool: Allows municipal surveyors to register new household service connections, public taps, and pipeline extensions with GPS accuracy and photos, subject to Municipal Engineer digital approval.
  • Geo-Fenced Plumber App (≤ 50m Lock): Plumbers cannot close water leak or burst tickets without standing within 50 meters of the leak coordinates and uploading live before-and-after excavation photos.

2.2 Citizen Supply Schedule Broadcasts

  • Automated WhatsApp and SMS alerts to citizens regarding scheduled water release hours per ward.
  • QR code stickers on public drinking water fountains for instant scan-to-report complaints.

3. IoT Hardware & Telemetry Standards

Telemetry Hardware Technical Specifications
Ultrasonic Level Sensors (OHT) Range: 0.3m to 10.0m; Accuracy: ±0.25% FS; IP68 submersible enclosure; Solar battery pack with 72-hour autonomy; 4G/NB-IoT cellular telemetry.
Pipeline Pressure Transmitters Range: 0 to 10 Bar; Stainless Steel 316L diaphragm; Burst pressure: 2× rated; Automated burst alarm mode upon >25% sudden pressure drop.
Sluice Valve Asset Plates Heavy-gauge anodized aluminum asset tags with engraved Valve ID, Ward Number, and Emergency Contact details.
