SECTION 1: CODE-MIXED TAMIL + ENGLISH DICTIONARY / கலப்பு மொழி புரிதல்
================================================================================

Citizens commonly speak colloquial Tamil mixed with English terms (Code-Switching).
The AI must treat all the following variations as identical:

1. STREET LIGHT PHRASES (தெரு விளக்கு சொற்கள்):
   - "street light eriyala" / "ஸ்ட்ரீட் லைட் எரியல" = Street light not working
   - "veetu pakkathula light eriyala" / "வீட்டு பக்கத்துல லைட் எரியல" = Street light issue near home
   - "lamp post break aayiduchu" / "லேம்ப் போஸ்ட் பிரேக் ஆயிடுச்சு" = Damaged electric pole
   - "wire dharala thoongudhu / cut aayiduchu" / "வயர் கட் ஆயிடுச்சு" = Broken wire (Emergency)
   - "light blinking / flickering aagudhu" / "லைட் பிளிங்க் ஆகுது" = Flickering light
   - "pole number / light number" = Pole ID

2. WATER SUPPLY PHRASES (குடிநீர் விநியோகச் சொற்கள்):
   - "water supply varala" / "வாட்டர் சப்ளை வரல" = No water supply
   - "water pressure low-aa irukku" / "வாட்டர் பிரஷர் லோ-வா இருக்கு" = Low pressure
   - "pipe leak aagudhu / pipeline burst" / "பைப் லீக் ஆகுது" = Pipeline leakage
   - "dirty water / mud water varudhu" / "டர்டி வாட்டர் வருது" = Contaminated water
   - "public tap broken" / "பப்ளிக் டேப் உடைஞ்சு போச்சு" = Broken public tap

3. GARBAGE & SANITATION PHRASES (குப்பை & துப்புரவு சொற்கள்):
   - "garbage collect pannala / vandi varala" / "கார்பேஜ் கலெக்ட் பண்ணல" = Missed waste collection
   - "dustbin overflow aagudhu" / "டஸ்ட்பின் ஓவர்ஃப்ளோ ஆகுது" = Overflowing bin
   - "street-la garbage dump pannirukaanga" / "குப்பை டம்பிங்" = Illegal garbage dump
   - "drainage block aayiduchu / drainage overflow" / "டிரைனேஜ் பிளாக்" = Blocked drainage
   - "dead dog / dead animal irukku" = Dead animal removal request

4. LOCATION & WARD PHRASES:
   - "veetu pakkathula" = Near my house / residence
   - "bus stand backside / near bus stand" = Ward 7
   - "GH road / hospital pakkam" = Ward 9
   - "Ooty road / tollgate area" = Ward 5
   - "station road / railway station" = Ward 6
   - "Karamadai road / bypass" = Ward 2


SECTION 2: MUNICIPAL SERVICES & CAPABILITIES / நகராட்சி சேவைகள்
================================================================================

1. STREET LIGHT MAINTENANCE (தெரு விளக்கு பராமரிப்பு)
- What we resolve: Non-working lights, dim/flickering lights, day-burning lights, leaning/damaged poles, sparking/snapped wires (emergency).
- SLA: Normal fixes 24-48 hours; Dangerous wires within 2 hours.

2. WATER SUPPLY SERVICES (குடிநீர் விநியோகம்)
- What we resolve: No water supply, low pressure, dirty/muddy water, pipeline leaks, broken public taps.
- Regular Timings: Morning 6:00 AM to 9:30 AM | Evening 4:30 PM to 7:00 PM.
- SLA: Pipeline burst within 4-12 hours; Normal issues 24-48 hours.

3. SOLID WASTE MANAGEMENT & SANITATION (திடக்கழிவு & துப்புரவு பணி)
- What we resolve: Missed garbage collection, overflowing dustbins, street dumping, dead animal removal, drainage block.
- Door-to-Door Timing: Daily morning 6:00 AM to 10:00 AM.
- SLA: Dead animal within 4 hours; Missed waste cleared same day.
